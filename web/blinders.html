Connect Chrome
  1. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Select the extension folder printed by blinders open.
  2. Click the Blinders extension on this inbox, then click it on the research tab. Return here to capture. You can also use its shortcut from Chrome’s extension shortcuts page.
  3. For text, first highlight the passage in the connected tab. For a screenshot, leave the desired view visible. Click the extension on another research tab to switch sources.
